## Analytics contract and limits

POST /api/analytics/v1/weekly takes strict version=1, kind=usage|workout, UTC Monday week, random UUID tag, positive bounded sequence and whitelisted integer counts. No Authorization, Cookie, proof header, query parameters or extra fields. Max body 4096 bytes; only current/previous week accepted. Reps round down to tens and minutes to five-minute blocks on device. Full history upload is retired at both actual client and consumer model.

Protected profile settings have two separate OFF-by-default adult-only consent epochs. Owner/current age/captured age/epoch must all match at completion or Watch import. Missing old context is excluded. Consent withdrawal clears unsent data immediately; temporary persistence failure remains visibly off and is retained in process for retry. Queue holds at most four weekly snapshots and 64 KiB including bounded local deduplication, with at most eight retry attempts and exponential backoff. Protected installation metadata reserves transmissions before a network attempt. Across all local profiles, each kind has at most fourteen current-week attempts and two additional previous-week final attempts, with at least twelve hours between reservations. Failed or ambiguous attempts conservatively consume their reservation. This can delay or undercount the service summaries; it never changes the personal reports based on saved local work. Analytics failures never block training or authentication.

Installation state shares only one random installation/week tag, a monotonic sequence and transmission-budget metadata. It contains no account/profile identifiers, counters or consent choices. Separate profiles do not share local counters or consents. Their snapshots replace the installation contribution, conservatively undercounting activity when profiles switch. Lower/out-of-order sequences cannot replace a newer snapshot. Tags count installations, not verified people. This is not a Sybil defense or anonymity guarantee.

D1 retains at most 10,000 rows, 16 accepted updates per tag/kind/week, and a conservative 3000 daily update budget. Expiry is 28 days from week start, excluded immediately from summaries and deleted at the next successful hourly maintenance. Each reported positive bucket independently requires ten contributing tags. Rare buckets stay suppressed even if ten installations contribute elsewhere. No raw events, identifiers, Health samples, exact workout timestamps or public raw endpoint. Application request logging remains disabled. Infrastructure delivery/security processing is separate and is disclosed.
