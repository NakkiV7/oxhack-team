Sub-millisecond Time-Series Analytics

A single 20-minute workout generates over 7,200 sensor samples (heart rate, triaxial acceleration, rotational velocity).

By utilizing TigerData's TimescaleDB continuous aggregate policies, Spottr rolls up time-bucketed statistics (`time_bucket('1 day', measured_at)`) automatically in the background, ensuring smooth and instant chart rendering on both mobile and web. 

SELECT
  time_bucket('3 days', measured_at) AS interval_bucket,
  kind,
  avg(value) AS avg_val,
  percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY value) AS median_val
FROM spottr_gateway.scalar_sample
WHERE installation_id = $1
  AND measured_at >= now() - INTERVAL '30 days'
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1 ASC;
