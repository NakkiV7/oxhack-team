Terms for Spottr training, accounts and Apple membership.

Updated 7 November 2026. Testing candidate

Spottr is currently in testing. It provides general fitness information and training tools. Camera counts, cues and new features may be incomplete or incorrect. The website is not a medical or emergency service.

Using Spottr

Spottr is developed by Omar Choudhry. Questions can be sent to O.Choudhry@leeds.ac.uk. Use the app and website lawfully, follow setup instructions, and choose activities appropriate for your circumstances.

You are responsible for the space, equipment, supervision and support needed for your exercise. Stop an activity if it causes pain or discomfort. Seek individual professional advice where you need help deciding whether an exercise is suitable.

Fitness guidance and tracking limits

Spottr is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation programme or substitute for a qualified professional. It does not promise health improvements, injury prevention, clinical accuracy or safe form.

Camera tracking estimates movement from visible joints. Exercise selection, body visibility, camera angle, lighting and surroundings can affect counts and cues. Use manual tracking where a movement is not supported or camera tracking is unsuitable. Recorded sets and estimates do not establish that an exercise was performed correctly.

Local coaching offers general suggestions, not individual clinical advice. An optional Watch’s workout or heart-rate information does not establish a medical assessment.

Testing availability

The public invitation opens Apple’s TestFlight service. Available places, supported devices, build availability and expiry are controlled by the testing programme and Apple. App screenshots on this site show a local testing candidate; the available TestFlight build may differ.

New features still require physical-device testing. Testing access does not guarantee continued availability, any specific future feature, or a paid membership. Keep any records important to you in a form you control.

Apple membership

The configured UK products are £10 per month with a 14-day introductory trial for eligible new subscribers, or £200 for a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple approval and availability remain pending. These plans are not a live purchase offer on this website. Products and introductory offers must first be available through Apple.

When a purchase is offered, Apple’s purchase screen will state the actual local price, billing period, trial eligibility and applicable terms before confirmation. Monthly subscriptions renew until cancelled under the displayed Apple terms. Lifetime describes access to the membership features included in that product; it does not promise every future product, third-party service or indefinite support for every device.

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription through your Apple account. Apple explains subscription cancellation. Deleting this website account does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles refund requests under its process and applicable consumer rights.

A website account does not unlock paid membership. Promotional codes are only valid if issued and enabled through the relevant Apple offer; no active promotional code is offered here.

Spottr accounts

An account and verified Apple membership are required to start workouts. Native setup uses Apple or verified email identity, then a passkey and Authenticator. Single-use backup codes can replace an Authenticator code. Recovery requires a linked verified identity and an unused backup code before replacing credentials. Protect your sign-in methods and keep recovery information somewhere private.

The website does not sync or back up your workout history, video or health information. If you lose all required sign-in and recovery methods, we may be unable to restore access. Do not attempt to access another person’s account or interfere with the service.

See Privacy for data handling and Delete account for deletion options.

Availability, changes and your rights

Testing features can change or be withdrawn as the product develops. We may restrict access to protect the service or respond to misuse. We will update these terms when the service changes.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable statutory consumer rights. If you have a concern, contact us so we can investigate.
