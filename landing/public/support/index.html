Help us understand the issue

Include your app version and build, iPhone model, iOS version, the exercise or screen involved, and the steps that led to the issue. For Watch issues, include the Watch model and watchOS version.

Check screenshots before sending: they can contain personal records. Please do not send health records, exercise videos, passkeys, Authenticator setup secrets, one-time codes or backup codes. Support is not an urgent or medical advice service.

Joining testing

Open the invitation with Apple’s TestFlight app installed. Apple controls available places and builds. The screenshots on this website show the current local testing candidate; the TestFlight build may differ, and some new features may not yet be distributed.

Camera counts look wrong

Choose a supported exercise and follow its camera-position guidance. Keep your whole body visible with clear lighting and room to move. Reposition your phone if joints disappear from view. Camera estimates can be wrong even with a good setup.

If tracking remains unsuitable, use manual reps or a timed option. Do not change an exercise in a way that causes pain just to satisfy the camera.

Offline training and local records

The core training and local coaching flow works offline on your iPhone. You do not need a website account. Your website account does not sync or restore local app history, lesson clips or health data.

Device and cloud backups may create separate copies under the settings you choose. Avoid removing the app if you still need its local records. For data or account removal, start with Delete account and data.

Signing in to the website

Where available, website accounts use a passkey and an Authenticator code. A backup code can replace the Authenticator factor once. A passkey is still required. Check that your device clock is set automatically if a current Authenticator code is rejected.

Do not share sign-in or recovery secrets with support. If every passkey or required recovery method is lost, access may not be recoverable. Visit Account for the current sign-in and account-management options.

Membership and Apple purchases

Membership prices on this website describe a proposed UK launch plan. There is no website checkout and no active tester offer advertised here. The app’s Apple purchase sheet will show actual availability, prices and eligibility.

When subscriptions are available, manage them through your Apple account. Read Apple’s cancellation instructions. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Privacy requests

For a privacy or deletion request, use the developer contact above and describe what you need. You do not need to send workout or health records to ask a question. Read Privacy for the service’s data handling.
