The details, when you need them.

How do my account and membership work?

Start with Apple or a verified email, then set up a passkey and Authenticator. Your account and verified Apple membership are required to start workouts. Trusted sessions persist so ordinary training does not require repeated sign-in.

Use the account page to manage the same account on the website. Existing website accounts remain supported. A website account does not unlock paid membership or back up completed workouts. TestFlight access does not establish a paid entitlement.

Apple handles purchases and subscriptions when the configured products become available. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting a Spottr account or buying lifetime access does not cancel a monthly subscription. No active promotional code is offered here. Get account and membership support.

Is this the app I can get on TestFlight?

The local beta candidate includes 53 exercises and 52 camera profiles, with manual and timed tracking options. These features may not all be in the public TestFlight build.

Screens show local profiles captured on 6–7 November 2026, including an illustrative manual test entry. New camera variations, Watch behaviour and coaching changes still need physical-device testing. These screens do not establish their accuracy.

How accurate is the camera tracking?

Camera tracking estimates movement from visible joints. Counts and cues can be wrong, especially when your body is out of frame or the angle is unsuitable. Follow the setup guidance, keep your whole body visible in clear lighting and use manual tracking when needed. Spottr does not claim clinical validation or guaranteed form assessment.

What stays on my iPhone?

Your workout history, video and raw Health data stay on your device. The core camera-training workflow processes frames locally. Your account does not back up completed workouts.

Adults can separately choose to save a programme configuration and opt in to coarse feature counts or weekly workout statistics. Those statistics are two independent choices, both off by default. Imported clips and personal reports stay local. See Privacy for details and backup considerations.

Can I use Spottr for rehabilitation?

Spottr provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device or a rehabilitation programme. Do not use it to diagnose, treat or monitor an injury or condition. Choose activities appropriate for you and stop if they cause pain or discomfort.
