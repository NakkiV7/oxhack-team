A few good questions.

Something else on your mind?
Visit support.

Can I use Spottr without an account?

Yes. Core iPhone training works offline without a website account. An optional website account does not sync your workout history, video or health data. Website accounts are separate from Apple purchases.

Is this the app I can get on TestFlight?

The images show the current local testing candidate captured on 5 November 2026. The available public TestFlight build may differ. The expanded catalogue and camera variations described here are local beta candidate features, not a claim that all are publicly distributed. New camera variations, Watch behavior and coaching changes still need physical-device testing; these screens do not establish their accuracy.

How accurate is the camera tracking?

Camera tracking estimates movement from visible joints. Counts and cues can be wrong, especially when your body is out of frame or the angle is unsuitable. Use supported setups and manual tracking when needed. Spottr does not claim clinical validation or guaranteed form assessment.

Does my video go to a server?

The core camera-training workflow processes frames on your device. This website has no video, workout or health upload feature. Imported lesson clips and locally saved training data remain separate from your website account. See Privacy for the details and backup considerations.

What is the optional website account for?

It is a separate account area using a passkey and an Authenticator code, with recovery backup codes. It is not required for the app and does not grant membership or a cloud workout backup. Visit the account page for its current availability.

Can I use Spottr for rehabilitation?

Spottr provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device or a rehabilitation programme. Do not use it to diagnose, treat or monitor an injury or condition. Choose activities appropriate for you and stop if they cause pain or discomfort.

How will purchases and cancellations work?

Membership is planned through Apple in-app purchases. The app will present Apple’s current terms before a purchase. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account; deleting a website account does not cancel one. No purchase or active promotional code is offered on this website.
