What do I need to start?

An account is required to start workouts, together with verified Apple membership. Set up using Apple or a verified email, then a passkey and Authenticator. Trusted sessions persist so ordinary training does not require repeated sign-in. Your history, video and raw health data stay on your device.

Is this the app I can get on TestFlight?

The images show local testing profiles captured on 6–7 November 2026, including an illustrative manual test entry. The available public TestFlight build may differ. The expanded catalogue and camera variations described here are local beta candidate features, not a claim that all are publicly distributed. New camera variations, Watch behavior and coaching changes still need physical-device testing; these screens do not establish their accuracy.

How accurate is the camera tracking?

Camera tracking estimates movement from visible joints. Counts and cues can be wrong, especially when your body is out of frame or the angle is unsuitable. Use supported setups and manual tracking when needed. Spottr does not claim clinical validation or guaranteed form assessment.

Does my video go to a server?

The core camera-training workflow processes frames on your device. Completed workout logs, video and raw Health readings are not uploaded. Adults can separately opt in to coarse feature counts and weekly workout statistics. Imported clips and personal reports stay local. See Privacy for the details and backup considerations.

Can I manage my account on the website?

Use your passkey and Authenticator to manage the same account on the account page. Existing website accounts remain supported. An account does not unlock paid membership or back up completed workouts. Adult profiles can separately choose to save a programme configuration.

Can I use Spottr for rehabilitation?

Spottr provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device or a rehabilitation programme. Do not use it to diagnose, treat or monitor an injury or condition. Choose activities appropriate for you and stop if they cause pain or discomfort.

How will purchases and cancellations work?

Membership uses Apple in-app purchases when the configured products become available. The app will present Apple’s current terms before a purchase. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account; deleting a Spottr account does not cancel one. No purchase or active promotional code is offered on this website.
