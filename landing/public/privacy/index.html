Privacy, clearly

How Spottr handles local training data, Spottr accounts and support information.

Updated 7 November 2026. Testing candidate

Your training stays separate. Training works offline after account setup and verified membership. Completed workout logs, video and raw Health data are not uploaded. Adult profiles can choose to save programme configurations and separately opt in to coarse statistics.

Who looks after this service?

Spottr is developed by Omar Choudhry. For privacy questions and requests, contact O.Choudhry@leeds.ac.uk, the contact published on Omar’s website. A university email address does not imply university endorsement or that the university operates Spottr.

This notice describes the new native training candidate and the connected Spottr account service. Older testing builds may have different demo or online-provider features; check the build and feature you use before sharing information.

On your iPhone

The core camera-training workflow analyses frames on your device to estimate visible joints and movement. Your local profile, saved training history, preferences and imported lesson clips are stored on the device. Core offline coaching does not send your coaching prompts to an online AI service.

Camera and health access are controlled by the permissions you grant in iOS or watchOS. Optional Apple Watch features use the relevant workout and heart-rate permissions. You can review or withdraw permissions in device settings. Withdrawing a permission does not automatically erase previously saved records.

New scoped training containers use protected, backup-excluded storage. Spottr has no app-owned CloudKit health store and does not upload raw Health data to its account or statistics services. Apple Health may sync or back up Health data through iCloud under your Apple settings and terms. Spottr’s app-container backup exclusions do not control Apple Health syncing. Older backups and files you explicitly export or share may still contain copies under your control. Excluding new containers does not erase earlier copies. A Spottr account is not a backup of completed workouts.

Optional Apple Health access

If you choose Connect Apple Health, Spottr can read the Health types you allow, including recorded resting and walking heart rate, heart-rate variability, cardio fitness, one-minute heart-rate recovery, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, steps and walking or running distance. Available readings show their measurement time and source. Historical Health readings are kept separate from live workout measurements and are not added to a session as if they were measured during it.

These snapshots are read and displayed on your iPhone, without sending them to this website or an AI service. With your permission, the adult Watch workout recorder also saves workouts and available measurements to Apple Health through HealthKit. Junior profiles do not collect or save HealthKit workouts in this release. Apple controls Health synchronisation and backups according to your device settings. See Apple’s Health data guidance. Your saved workout reports and temporary paired-device sync queues may contain Watch heart rate, active energy and wrist-motion summaries. You can review Health access in iOS Settings and clear local training data in Spottr. Optional maximum-heart-rate settings are stored locally for estimated zones; they do not change Apple Watch zone settings.

Spottr accounts

Native setup verifies Apple identity or an email ownership code, then requires a passkey and Authenticator. We store an account identifier, passkey public keys and counters, encrypted Authenticator information, hashed backup codes, linked provider identifiers and encrypted Apple grants needed for revocation. Email ownership identifiers are keyed hashes. Temporary email verification data is encrypted and expires after five minutes. Resend delivers ownership codes using its free service. Apple processes Sign in with Apple under its own privacy terms.

Your passkey private key stays with your device or passkey provider. We do not receive fingerprint or face scans. Native device sessions use signed requests with short access tokens and rotating refresh credentials held in protected device storage. Access expires after 15 minutes and refresh sessions expire after 180 days without renewal. Recovery needs a freshly verified linked identity and unused backup code. Website sign-in uses the same passkey and Authenticator. Legacy website accounts continue to work.

Adult profiles may independently choose to save a programme configuration. The service stores a random profile slot, revision and permitted goals, support and exercise schedule. It does not store age, body measurements, Health readings or completed workout history. Withdrawal stops new saves and removes the saved copy when connected. Local export remains available.

For requested account and programme services we rely on UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b). For proportionate security and support we rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), including preventing abuse and resolving problems.

Optional coarse statistics

Adults have two independent choices, both off by default. Feature usage sends coarse counts of named features. Workout statistics sends weekly session and set totals, rounded reps and minutes, movement-family and recording-source counts, and broad effort bins. No analytics is collected for under-18 or unknown-age profiles. Changing consent clears unsent data and stops future collection. A later opt-in does not upload earlier workouts.

Requests use a separate client without account cookies or authorization. They exclude account and profile identifiers, email, age, date of birth, body measurements, BMI, raw Health or heart-rate readings, energy, precise workout times, free text, camera frames, audio and video. A random weekly installation tag is transient pseudonymous processing, not a guarantee of anonymity. It stops being used after 28 days from the week starting and is erased by the next successful hourly cleanup. A delayed cleanup may retain expired rows longer, but they are excluded from summaries. Repeated snapshots replace earlier totals. Switching consenting profiles and bounded service delivery can conservatively delay or undercount an installation's contribution. Personal reports continue to use your own saved local work.

Only summaries with at least ten contributing installations in each reported bucket are surfaced. Rare buckets remain suppressed even when the overall week qualifies. This means installations, not verified people, and is not a formal anonymity guarantee. There is no public raw-statistics endpoint, advertising SDK or application request logging. Already combined summaries cannot be attributed back to an account for deletion. Optional statistics rely on consent under Article 6(1)(a).

Hosting, cookies and security

This website is hosted through Cloudflare. Delivering and protecting the site involves technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested paths and timestamps. Cloudflare may process data across its international infrastructure. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its own processing information.

Spottr does not add third-party advertising, analytics or marketing cookies to this site. Account features use necessary first-party security and session cookies. These support sign-in and request protection rather than advertising. Static public pages do not need an account cookie to be read.

Account records are retained until you delete the account. Website sessions expire after 12 hours. Enrollment, sign-in and Authenticator-replacement challenges expire after 5 minutes and permit at most five attempts. Expired records are cleaned up when account requests run and by scheduled maintenance; expiry does not depend on that cleanup having run. Security rate limits use secret-key-hashed IP identifiers in 10-minute buckets, rather than storing a raw IP address in those rate-limit records. Hosting security records and backup copies can have separate retention under Cloudflare’s operational policies. Deleting an active account does not erase every infrastructure backup immediately.

Purchases, testing and support

If you use TestFlight or make an Apple purchase when products become available, Apple processes information under its own terms and privacy notice. Spottr does not receive your payment-card details through this website. Read Apple’s privacy policy.

If you contact support, we receive the contact information and content you choose to send. We use this to answer your request and investigate the issue, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or a legal requirement. Please avoid sending videos, health records, private credentials or recovery codes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask about access, correction, deletion and other applicable data-protection rights using the contact above. Rights depend on the circumstances. We may need enough information to verify that a request relates to your account, but you should never email a passkey, Authenticator secret or backup code.

Use Delete account for the account process and the separate steps for local app data and Apple subscriptions. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

We will update this page when the service’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this notice.
