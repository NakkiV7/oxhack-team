Privacy, clearly

How Spottr handles local training data, optional website accounts and support information.

Updated 5 November 2026 · Testing candidate

Your training stays separate. The core app works offline. This website has no workout, camera-video or health upload feature. Creating a website account does not create a cloud training history.

Who looks after this service?

Spottr is developed by Omar Choudhry. For privacy questions and requests, contact O.Choudhry@leeds.ac.uk, the contact published on Omar’s website. A university email address does not imply university endorsement or that the university operates Spottr.

This notice describes the current offline testing candidate and the optional website account service. Older testing builds may have different demo or online-provider features; check the build and feature you use before sharing information.

On your iPhone

The core camera-training workflow analyses frames on your device to estimate visible joints and movement. Your local profile, saved training history, preferences and imported lesson clips are stored on the device. Core offline coaching does not send your coaching prompts to an online AI service.

Camera and health access are controlled by the permissions you grant in iOS or watchOS. Optional Apple Watch features use the relevant workout and heart-rate permissions. You can review or withdraw permissions in device settings. Withdrawing a permission does not automatically erase previously saved records.

Your device, iCloud or computer backups, and files you export or share can create separate copies under your control. Those copies are governed by the settings and provider you choose. A website account is not a backup of your app data.

Optional website accounts

If account creation is enabled, the account service stores a generated account identifier, your chosen account name, passkey credential identifiers, public keys and sign counters, encrypted Authenticator setup information, hashed single-use backup codes, and hashed session-token or verification records. It does not require your email address, a password, video, workout or health information.

Your passkey’s private key stays with your device or passkey provider; this website does not receive your fingerprint or face scan. An Authenticator code is used as a second factor. Every new sign-in requires a passkey plus an Authenticator code. A backup code can replace the Authenticator factor once, but a passkey is still required. Keep passkeys and recovery information private.

For the account service you request, our lawful basis is performance of a contract under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b): the account details are needed to create and manage that service. For proportionate security and support processing, we rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f). Those interests are keeping accounts and the service secure, preventing abuse, and understanding and resolving support requests, balanced against your rights and interests. The account page shows the currently enabled features. Creating an account does not provide Apple purchase rights or sync your training data.

Hosting, cookies and security

This website is hosted through Cloudflare. Delivering and protecting the site involves technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested paths and timestamps. Cloudflare may process data across its international infrastructure. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its own processing information.

Spottr does not add third-party advertising, analytics or marketing cookies to this site. Account features use necessary first-party security and session cookies. These support sign-in and request protection rather than advertising. Static public pages do not need an account cookie to be read.

Account records are retained until you delete the account. Website sessions expire after 12 hours. Enrollment, sign-in and Authenticator-replacement challenges expire after 5 minutes and permit at most five attempts. Expired records are cleaned up when account requests run and by scheduled maintenance; expiry does not depend on that cleanup having run. Security rate limits use secret-key-hashed IP identifiers in 10-minute buckets, rather than storing a raw IP address in those rate-limit records. Hosting security records and backup copies can have separate retention under Cloudflare’s operational policies. Deleting an active account does not erase every infrastructure backup immediately.

Purchases, testing and support

If you use TestFlight or make an Apple purchase when products become available, Apple processes information under its own terms and privacy notice. Spottr does not receive your payment-card details through this website. Read Apple’s privacy policy.

If you contact support, we receive the contact information and content you choose to send. We use this to answer your request and investigate the issue, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or a legal requirement. Please avoid sending videos, health records, private credentials or recovery codes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask about access, correction, deletion and other applicable data-protection rights using the contact above. Rights depend on the circumstances. We may need enough information to verify that a request relates to your account, but you should never email a passkey, Authenticator secret or backup code.

Use Delete account for the website-account process and the separate steps for local app data and Apple subscriptions. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

We will update this page when the service’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this notice.
