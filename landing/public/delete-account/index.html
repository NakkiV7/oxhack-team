Your account. Your choice.

Delete a Spottr account and understand the separate steps for local app data and Apple subscriptions.

Updated 7 November 2026. Testing candidate

Three separate things: your Spottr account, your local app data, and an Apple subscription. Removing one does not automatically remove or cancel the others.

Delete your Spottr account

  1. Open Account and sign in with your passkey and Authenticator code, or a valid single-use backup code.
  2. Choose the account-deletion option in account management.
  3. Deletion requires a sign-in completed within the last 5 minutes. If asked to sign in again, use your passkey and second factor, return to Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm.

You can also open Account and profiles in the app and choose Delete online account. Confirmed deletion removes the active account, saved programme configurations, linked identities, sign-in and recovery records, and revokes linked Apple grants. Local phone and Watch records are preserved. This does not erase separate copies held in hosting backups immediately.

If the account service or deletion option is unavailable, contact O.Choudhry@leeds.ac.uk for a deletion request. We may need to verify ownership. Never send your passkey, Authenticator secret or backup codes by email.

Remove local app data

The website has no copy of your local workout history, profile, lesson videos or health data to delete. Website-account deletion does not remove those records from your iPhone or Watch.

To remove the app’s local container, delete the app from the device rather than choosing Offload App. Keep any records you need before doing so. Separately review files you exported, device or cloud backups, and any information saved in Apple Health, because those can survive app removal.

You control Apple Health records and permissions through Apple’s Health and device settings. For help identifying the relevant data, contact support without sending the records themselves.

Cancel an Apple subscription separately

Deleting your website account or app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Open your iPhone’s Settings, select your Apple account, then Subscriptions, and manage the relevant subscription if one exists.

Read Apple’s subscription-cancellation instructions. The configured membership products still require Apple approval and availability.

Privacy and retained copies

Account removal concerns active Spottr account records. Optional coarse statistics use no account identifier. Withdrawal clears unsent statistics and stops future collection. Previously combined summaries cannot be attributed back to an account. Hosting security information, legally required records or infrastructure backups may follow separate retention. You can ask about a specific request through the developer contact above. Read Privacy for the full context.
