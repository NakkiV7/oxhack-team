Unauthenticated image access returned 401. Explicit stop returned 200; subsequent authenticated frame request returned 503. Final health was `camera: stopped`, `frame_available: false`. Certificate-validated HTTPS health requests from the Mac to the Pi took 16.8–76.1 ms across five samples, including fresh TLS connections. The local certificate's DER hash matched the saved pin.

## Sustained-capture blocker

Direct ffmpeg diagnostics isolated the failure below HTTP: MJPEG initially produced frames, then `VIDIOC_DQBUF: No such device`; kernel logs showed USB disconnects and new device numbers. A trial with per-packet flushing did not establish a fix and no flushing change was applied.

One advertised lower-resolution alternative, NV12 1280×720 at 30 fps with the same 640×360/10 fps output, produced 11 frames. Its last frame arrived 2.03 seconds after start, then the USB device disappeared and ffmpeg reported the same device error. No USB reset, kernel option, boot/power setting or camera firmware change was attempted. `vcgencmd get_throttled` reported `0x0` and recent kernel logs contained no voltage/over-current warnings. The underlying cable, power, device or firmware cause remains unproven and requires physical diagnosis before claiming continuous tracking.
