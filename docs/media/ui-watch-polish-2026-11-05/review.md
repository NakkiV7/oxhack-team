Actual unedited captures from the existing shared iPhone simulator, 5 November 2026. Test storage is isolated. No presentation history, physiology or successful Watch connection was seeded.

Reviewed the onboarding, Today, Coach, Skills, Watch milestones, supported-squat artwork and the largest accessibility text category used by the UI test. The workout image treatment is consistent, supported squats retain the rail, coach selection is visible, and the accessibility selector replaces cramped segments. Actions remain reachable by scrolling. At the largest text size, labels wrap and content spans multiple screens. The system glass navigation/tab materials blur content passing behind them during scrolling.
