# Spottr pitch recording checklist

Pitch to OneMedicalGroup. The proposal is £125,000 for 30 percent to develop the MVP and explore a GP-practice social-prescribing pilot. Record Spottr 1.0 build 27 on the iPhone with a dedicated training profile and real session inputs. The [build 27 delivery receipt](evidence/spottr-build27-delivery-2026-11-10.md) records the installation.

1. Complete sign-in, passkey and Authenticator setup before recording. Keep codes, backup information, private identity and measurements out of footage. Open a planned workout before the recording session to confirm the account has the necessary Apple sandbox or TestFlight membership access.
2. Show Today with the saved complete weekly plan and a recovery day. Reopen it to show that the plan stays stable.
3. Perform a short real camera set with the faceless joint figure and independently count repetitions. Demonstrate brief occlusion only if the observed trial works; retain the continuous original and disclose edited excerpts.
4. Turn the camera off, show preserved session quantities and enter an actual manual set result and effort. Let the automatic rest run.
5. Show the real Watch metrics and controls only after hardware checks pass. For phone-free use, start a genuinely cached workout, log sets/rest, then reconnect and confirm one copy appears in phone history. Do not use simulator heart rate.
6. Open the saved report and actual earned skill/quest progress. Distinguish completed work, manual input, missing sensors and existing placement.
7. Finish on the abstract coach choice and the two independent optional analytics controls. Describe local personal reports separately from coarse service statistics.

A concise 90-second cut can allocate 15 seconds to the plan, 30 to a real set, 15 to camera and manual continuity, 15 to Watch sync and 15 to the report and progression. If Watch sync is unavailable, use that time to show the manual set and the finished report. Keep an offline copy of the chosen recording with the deck and check its audio on the presentation machine.

Describe clinical recovery and GP prescribing as proposed pilot work. The pitch can show general fitness features in build 27. Refer to the £10 monthly plan with the eligible 14-day trial and £200 lifetime plan as configured UK pricing while App Store review is pending. Advertise tester-code availability only after a real code is active.
