# Spottr offline MVP

Build on the existing SwiftUI iPhone and Watch app. The user authorises implementation across the app, with computer vision and Watch feedback as the focus. Consumer flows must have no generative AI, provider tokens or paid runtime services. Existing celebrity recordings remain optional; new original male and female coaches have an adjustable gentle-to-direct style and varied bundled recordings. ElevenLabs is an authoring tool only. Preserve the audio toggle and remember choices.

Add wall, incline, knee and close-grip push-ups, half, supported and wide squats. Use variation-specific joint geometry, stable top/depth hysteresis and bounded occlusion continuity. Never infer an entirely unseen rep. Camera interruptions and switching between camera and manual keep reps, elapsed work and completed sets. Speech counting is optional, requires on-device recognition, counts increasing spoken numbers once, and stays off while camera is active. Missing permissions never prevent manual entry.

Ask goals (including weight management), schedule, comfortable exercises and coach during onboarding. Store self-reported starting skills separately from recorded achievements. Show those skills as starting placements in a single vertical game path with upcoming locks and hidden future content. Remove consumer AI path generation, free-text coach and TestFlight preview. Old saved data is preserved; a new onboarding revision reopens setup once without deleting real history.

Insights derive from actual counts, active time, training days, rep timing and fresh Watch readings, with transparent denominators and source labels. No invented calories, weight loss or fitness scores. Coach uses options, local plans and deterministic feedback. Reduce duplicated goal labels and punctuation clutter. Watch offers distinct workout, controls and vitals pages with Crown navigation and freshness-aware metrics.

Use SSD scratch space, preserve signed build 17 and active dependencies, no Chrome or new simulators. Verify shared behavior and native compilation; real phone/Watch acceptance is separate. Add an ordered feature and testing backlog after implementation.

No human-style faces anywhere in the app, including coach selection. Use abstract colour and waveform coach designs. Preserve the existing faceless stickman exercise style and highlight relevant joints. No generated human anatomy/portrait artwork is used.
